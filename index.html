GOOD QUESTIONS

A clearer picture
before you begin.

Can I upgrade my existing alarm system?

Start with an assessment of its model, age, condition and current monitoring agreement. Some systems can be upgraded; others may need replacement components. Compatibility is confirmed before a quote.

Do I need a monthly membership?

The proposed Connected Care support membership is optional. Professional monitoring and some cloud features may require separate paid plans. Your quote should identify every recurring fee.

Will outdoor cameras work through winter?

The operating temperature, power supply and mounting location all matter. Solar battery cameras can stop charging in freezing conditions. A site assessment should establish a suitable power and maintenance plan.

Can lights and leak sensors work together?

Compatible systems can combine notifications with useful routines, such as flashing a light after a leak alert. The hub, devices and offline behaviour must be tested together. Alerts do not guarantee damage prevention.

Is this the official Modern Security website?

No. This is a proposed redesign for discussion with the owner. Packages, products, prices and memberships are illustrative. No orders or enquiries are sent from this concept.
